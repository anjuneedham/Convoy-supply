## Sending Tips

- **Personalize:** Find supplier's specific contact or sales manager (LinkedIn, company website). Avoid generic "hello" emails.
- **Be specific:** Mention exact material grades (1680D ballistic, not just "nylon"). Shows you know what you want.
- **Set hard dates:** "by Friday, Sept 27" beats "ASAP." Manufacturers respect deadlines.
- **Volume signal:** Mention 200-500 units/month growth potential so they treat you seriously (but don't exaggerate).
- **Follow up:** If no response in 3 days, send a polite reminder. Many suppliers ignore first contact.
