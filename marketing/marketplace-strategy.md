# ConvoySupply — Facebook Marketplace Strategy

**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Channel weight:** ~20% of organic effort
**Cost:** listing time only. No ad spend. **The cheapest channel in the plan.**

---

## Policy compliance — read first, and treat as binding

Marketplace bans accounts quickly and permanently, and an appeal is not a plan. Every rule below is non-negotiable.

| Rule | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| **Never misrepresent location** | List from where we actually ship. Never claim a local presence we do not have. |
| **Never misrepresent availability** | If it is out of stock, the listing comes down. No "message me for stock." |
| **Never list an item we cannot deliver** | No listing before inventory is physically in hand. |
| **Never use fake or deceptive images** | Real photographs of the real product. No AI product renders, no competitor photos, no stock images. |
| **Use shipping-enabled listings honestly** | If the listing says ships, it ships. Our listings are shipping-enabled, not local pickup. |
| **No off-platform payment solicitation before a legitimate handoff** | Directing a buyer to our own Shopify store is legitimate where it is transparent and the buyer chooses. Never ask for Venmo/Zelle/Cash App to dodge fees. |
| **No duplicate spam listings** | One listing per product per account. Renew rather than duplicate. |
| **Follow commerce policies for the category** | Vehicle accessories are permitted. Keep it that way. |

**Read Facebook's Commerce Policies before the first listing and re-read them if anything changes.** Nothing in this document overrides them.

## Why Marketplace is in the plan

Three reasons, in order:

1. **Auto parts and accessories is a named major Marketplace category** **[V1]** — the buyers are already there for this kind of product.
2. **Shipped items in the $10–$50 band move fastest** **[V1]** — which is exactly where CS-02 ($29.99) and CS-03 ($27.99) sit, and close to where CS-01 can be positioned.
3. **It costs nothing but time.** Every other channel in this plan consumes budget. If Marketplace produces even a handful of sales, it changes the unit economics materially because there is no CPA attached.

**The honest risk:** Marketplace skews local-pickup and our audience is mobile — a driver browsing in Ohio may be registered in Georgia. Whether shipped listings get meaningful impressions for this category is **unproven** and is one of the six questions the 30-day test answers. See R4 in `research/selected-niche.md`.

## The posting process

### Setup (once)
1. Use a real personal account with real history. **Do not create a burner** — Marketplace penalizes new accounts hard.
2. Create the ConvoySupply Facebook **Page** first. Buyers check the seller.
3. Enable Marketplace shipping (Commerce Manager → shipping settings). Understand the fee structure before listing.
4. Photograph everything before the first listing. **No listing without real photos of real product in hand.**

### Weekly rhythm
| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Post or renew all 5 listings. Marketplace favors fresh listings, so renewing beats leaving them to age. |
| **Daily, 3×** | Morning, midday, evening: clear the message queue |
| **Friday** | Log inquiries, replies, sales and conversion rate in `execution/metrics.md` |

**Target: reply within 1 hour during waking hours.** Marketplace buyers message three sellers and buy from whoever answers first. Response time is the single biggest controllable variable on this channel.

### Renewal, not duplication
Renew or edit the existing listing. **Never post duplicates to game the feed** — it is the fastest way to get flagged.

## Response scripts

Adapt, don't paste robotically. Buyers can tell.

### "Is this available?"
> Yes, in stock and ready to ship. Want me to check the fit against your truck first? Just tell me what you're driving.

*Answers the question, then immediately moves to the real objection. Do not just say "yes" — that ends the conversation.*

### "Will it fit my [truck]?"
> `[Model]` — yes, that's one we've confirmed. It needs a seat back at least 20" tall and 14" wide with a bit of clearance behind the headrest, and a `[model]` is comfortably over that.
>
> If it turns out not to work, send it back within 30 days.

If the model is **not** confirmed:
> Haven't confirmed that one specifically. Measure your seat back — height from the cushion to the base of the headrest, and width at the widest point. If it's over 20" × 14" it'll fit. Happy to talk it through if you send the numbers.

**Never guess a fit.** A wrong "yes" becomes a return, a bad experience, and possibly a public complaint.

### "Where are you located?"
> I'm in `[ACTUAL CITY, STATE]` and everything ships from here — this isn't a local pickup listing. Usually `[TBC]` business days to ship, then `[TBC]` in transit.
>
> If you're on the road I can ship to a terminal or a hold-for-pickup location instead. Just tell me where you'll actually be.

**State the real location. Always.** This script also turns a routine question into our strongest differentiator.

### "Can I pick it up?"
> These are shipping only — no local pickup. But if you're going to be somewhere specific I can ship it there, terminal included.

### "Will you take $30?" (on a $49.99 item)
> Can't do $30 on that one. I can do $44.99, or if you want the bunk net with it I'll do both for $70 instead of $79.98.

*Counter once with a real number, then pivot to a bundle. The bundle counter is the highest-value move available — it protects the unit price and raises AOV at the same time.*

### Lowball — "Will you take $15?"
> Appreciate the offer but that's below what I can do. $44.99 is my best on that one. It'll be here if you change your mind.

**Polite, firm, one sentence, no justification.** Do not explain costs — it reads as defensive and invites another round.

### "How long is shipping?"
> `[TBC]` business days to get it out, then `[TBC]` in transit. I'll send you tracking. If you need it by a date, tell me and I'll say honestly whether it'll make it.

### "Is this the same as the one on Amazon?"
> No. Most of those are car organizers listed under truck keywords — the straps don't reach a Class 8 seat. This is built to Class 8 dimensions and I publish every measurement. Happy to send them if you want to compare.

### "Do you have a website?"
> Yeah — `[URL]`. Full specs, dimensions and the other pieces are on there. Marketplace price on this one is a bit lower though, so if it's just this item you're better off here.

**Honest and it costs us nothing.** Telling a buyer they get a better price where they already are builds more trust than the $5 is worth.

### After a sale
> Thanks — shipping `[day]`, I'll send tracking. Anything doesn't work out, message me first before anything else. I'd rather sort it than have you stuck with it.

### Follow-up (7 days after delivery)
> Hey — did the `[product]` work out in your truck?
>
> If it did and you've got a minute, a review would genuinely help — we're new and we've got none yet. If it didn't, tell me what's wrong and I'll fix it.

**Send once. Never twice.** A second follow-up is a nuisance and this audience will say so.

## Handling difficult situations

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| **No-show / ghosted after agreeing** | Nothing. No follow-up. Relist. |
| **Wants to pay off-platform** | "I'd rather keep it through Marketplace or my site — that way you're covered if anything goes wrong." **Never accept Venmo/Zelle/Cash App to dodge fees.** |
| **Aggressive or abusive** | Stop responding. Block. Do not engage. |
| **Public complaint** | Answer once, honestly, publicly. Then move to DM. Never argue in a thread. |
| **Asks for a spec we don't have** | "I don't have a tested number for that. I'd rather tell you that than make one up." |
| **Wants it same-day** | "Can't do that — it ships, and that takes `[TBC]` days. If you need it today, a truck stop is your better bet." |

That last one loses a sale on purpose. Sending someone to a truck stop when that genuinely serves them better is the kind of thing drivers remember and repeat.

## Measurement

Log weekly in `execution/metrics.md`:

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Listings live | Consistency check |
| Views per listing | Is the channel giving us impressions at all? |
| Inquiries | Is the listing working? |
| **Inquiry → sale rate** | Are the scripts working? |
| Sales and revenue | The point |
| Median response time | The variable we most control |
| Most-asked question | Feeds the FAQ and the ad copy |

**Kill criterion:** 30+ listing-weeks across 21 days with **zero sales** = the channel is not viable for these goods. Stop and reallocate the time. This is failure condition 4 in `research/selected-niche.md`.

**Scale criterion:** if Marketplace inquiry→sale exceeds 15% and produces 5+ sales in the first 21 days, it becomes the primary channel and the paid budget gets reconsidered. A CPA-free channel that converts changes the entire economics.

## What Marketplace teaches us that ads cannot

Every inquiry is a free customer interview. Log the questions.

- The most-asked question becomes the top FAQ on the PDP
- The most common objection becomes an ad angle
- The truck models people actually name go into the fit guide
- The language buyers use goes into the copy
