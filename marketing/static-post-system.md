# ConvoySupply — Static Post System

**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Count:** 30 concepts — 10 problem · 5 educational · 5 benefit · 3 comparison · 3 lifestyle · 2 social-proof-style · 2 promotional
**Format:** static images and carousels. No video required for any concept here.

---

## How this system works

Each concept is a **template**, not a one-off. A concept can be re-shot, re-angled, and re-run for months. Thirty concepts at 5 posts a week is six weeks of content before anything repeats, and repetition with a new image is normal and effective.

**Field definitions**
- **Hook** — the on-image headline. It has to work with the sound off, the caption collapsed, and at thumbnail size.
- **Visual** — what is actually in frame.
- **Prompt** — an AI image-generation prompt. **AI is used for backgrounds, contexts, and problem states only.** Any frame containing our actual product must be a real photograph. See `brand/brand-strategy.md`.
- **Objective** — awareness · engagement · education · trust · traffic · conversion

**Design constants for every static post**
Asphalt or concrete background · Barlow Condensed uppercase headline · hi-viz amber for one emphasis only · wordmark bottom-right at 60% opacity · 4:5 portrait for feed, 1:1 for Marketplace and grid · text legible at 200px wide.

**The 200px test:** shrink the image to thumbnail width. If the hook is unreadable, the post fails. Most competitor creative fails this.

## Production system

**Batch, don't improvise.** One shoot day produces most of the real photography for all 30 concepts.

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1. Cab access | Borrow or rent access to a real Class 8 sleeper for one day. **This is the single most important production dependency in the whole plan.** |
| 2. Shoot list | Every concept marked REAL PHOTOGRAPH REQUIRED, plus every problem-state frame you can capture authentically |
| 3. Before/after | Tripod. Do not move the camera between frames. Shoot "before" first, genuinely messy. |
| 4. AI fills gaps | Backgrounds, contexts, and problem states only — never the product |
| 5. Template | One Canva or Figma template. Swap image + hook. 30 posts in an afternoon. |
| 6. Export | 4:5 feed · 1:1 Marketplace and grid · under 300 KB |
| 7. 200px test | Shrink to thumbnail. Unreadable hook = rejected. |

## Posting cadence

5 posts a week. Rotate categories so the feed never reads as a catalog:

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem | Educational | Benefit | Lifestyle or Comparison | Problem or Promotional |

**Ratio target: roughly 4 non-selling posts to 1 selling post.** A feed that sells every day gets scrolled past by this audience. Full schedule in `marketing/organic-content-calendar.md`.

## Measurement

Log weekly in `execution/metrics.md`: reach, engagement rate, saves, shares, comments, link clicks, and any attributed sessions.

**Saves and shares matter more than likes here.** A save means "I'll need this," which is the strongest organic purchase signal available. If the educational posts out-save the product posts, lean harder into education — that is a finding, not a failure.

## Rules — every post, no exceptions

1. No fabricated reviews, testimonials, or customer quotes
2. No fake urgency, countdowns, or expiring offers that don't expire
3. No safety, medical, compliance, or legal claims
4. No AI-generated image of our actual product presented as product photography
5. No customer photo reposted without explicit permission
6. No competitor brand marks or names in comparison content
7. No spec figure published that a supplier spec sheet doesn't support
8. Every claim in a caption must be true and checkable
