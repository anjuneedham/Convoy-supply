What it does

Straps to the passenger seat back. Deep bottom pockets for bulk. Elastic sleeves for cables, tools and a flashlight. A padded slot for a tablet. A flat document pocket that fits a clipboard without folding it.

How it works

Two cam-buckle straps over the seat back, one anchor strap behind the headrest. About two minutes. No tools. Nothing drilled, nothing bolted — so nothing to clear with your carrier, and it comes with you to the next truck.
