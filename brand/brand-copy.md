## Compliance rules — apply to every line in this repo

Hard rules. A line that breaks one of these does not ship, regardless of how well it performs.

1. **No fabricated reviews, testimonials, ratings, or customer quotes.** None. Anywhere.
2. **No fake urgency or scarcity.** No countdowns, no "only 3 left" unless it is literally true and system-driven.
3. **No compare-at price** unless the higher price was genuinely offered. At launch that means **bundles only**, against the true sum of individual prices.
4. **No safety, medical, compliance, or legal claims.** CS-03 organizes documents; it does not help you pass an inspection. Say "keeps loose items secured," never "prevents injury."
5. **No guarantee we cannot fund.** 30-day returns, stated plainly. No "lifetime warranty."
6. **No claims about materials or specs that supplier documentation does not support.** Every spec figure in customer-facing copy must trace to a supplier spec sheet. Until samples and spec sheets arrive, spec numbers in this repo are **placeholders marked [TBC]**.
7. **No fabricated press mentions, awards, or "as seen in."**
8. **No implying we hold inventory we do not hold**, and no implying local pickup on Marketplace if we are shipping.

## Voice check — run before publishing anything

1. Could this sentence sell a phone case? → Rewrite.
2. Is there an adjective where a number would work? → Use the number.
3. Does it claim something we cannot substantiate? → Cut it.
4. Would a 19-year driver think this was written by someone who's sat in a sleeper? → If not, rewrite.
5. Is there fake urgency, a fake review, or a fake price? → Remove it. No exceptions.
