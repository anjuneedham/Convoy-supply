**Purpose:** the destination for paid Meta traffic. Structured as a funnel, not as a product page.

---

## Why this is not the PDP

The PDP at `/products/cs-01-cab-command-organizer` serves visitors who already have context — they came from a collection, a search, or the cart. It is optimized for browsing and comparison.

This page serves a stranger who tapped an ad ten seconds ago and knows nothing. It has a different job:

| | PDP | Landing page |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor | Has context | Cold |
| Navigation | Full nav | **Logo only. No nav, no footer links.** |
| Structure | Product info architecture | Linear argument |
| Exits | Many (by design) | **One: add to cart** |
| Order | Product → details | **Problem → solution → proof → offer** |

Both exist. **Which converts paid traffic better is an open question and is tested as EXP-007** in `execution/experiments.md`.

---

## Page rules

- **No header nav, no footer nav.** Logo links home; nothing else leaves the page.
- **Sticky mobile add-to-cart bar** appears once the visitor scrolls past Section 3.
- **CTA repeats five times** — Sections 1, 4, 5, 8, 10. Every CTA goes to the same place with the same wording.
- **Mobile-first.** Facebook traffic is overwhelmingly mobile and this audience is frequently on poor signal. Target under 1.2 MB total.
- **No timers, no fake scarcity, no fabricated reviews.** The funnel structure does the work.

Track per section:

| Metric | Target **[H]** | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Scroll depth to §2 | >80% | Hook works |
| Scroll depth to §8 | >35% | Argument holds |
| LPV → ATC | >3% | Offer lands |

| Bundle share of orders | >30% | AOV lever working |

Targets are hypotheses, not benchmarks, and get replaced with real data in `execution/metrics.md`.

**Diagnostic logic:**

| Symptom | Read |
|---|---|
| High §2 scroll, low §8 scroll | Argument breaks between Solution and Offer — look at §5/§6 |
| High ATC, low checkout completion | Shipping cost or a checkout problem, not a page problem |
| Low ATC despite deep scroll | They believe the problem and not the offer — price or fit confidence |
| Bounce before §2 | Ad-to-page mismatch. Fix the ad, not the page. |
