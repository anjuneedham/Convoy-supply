# Returns & Refunds Policy

*Last updated: [DATE — fill in on publish]*

## The honest version

Because each order ships directly from the manufacturer rather than from a warehouse we control, mailing a product back isn't practical the way it would be with a traditional retailer — return shipping often costs more than the item itself. So instead of a mail-it-back policy, here's what we actually do.

## If your item arrives damaged, defective, or wrong

Email us within **30 days of delivery** with:
- A photo of the item and any damage/defect
- Your order number

We'll send a **replacement or a full refund** — whichever makes sense for the situation. In most cases, **you do not need to ship anything back.**

## If it just doesn't work for you

If the item fits your truck fine and arrived as described, but it's not what you expected or you changed your mind:

Email us within **14 days of delivery**. We'll work with you on a solution — a partial refund, store credit toward a different product, or in some cases a full refund, evaluated case by case. Since we can't always take physical items back, we're straightforward with you about what's possible before you order: **check the dimensions on the product page against your seat/bunk before buying.**

## What voids this

- Items reported more than 30 days after delivery
- Items that were clearly used beyond fitting/testing
- No order number or no way to verify the purchase

## Order cancellations

Since orders are processed quickly (often within 1–3 business days), email us **immediately** if you need to cancel. If it hasn't shipped yet, we'll cancel and refund in full. If it's already shipped, it falls under the return process above.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within **5–10 business days** of approval. Your bank or PayPal may take a few additional days to post it.

## Why we're built this way

Most of what we sell is inexpensive enough that a traditional "ship it back for inspection" process would cost more in shipping than the refund itself. We'd rather solve the problem directly than make you pay to mail something back. If that trade-off doesn't work for you, we understand — the specs and dimensions on every product page are there so you can check fit before ordering, not after.
