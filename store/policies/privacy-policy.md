## What this covers

This policy explains what information ConvoySupply collects when you visit our site or place an order, and what we do with it.

## Information we collect

**When you place an order:**
- Name, shipping address, email, phone number
- Payment information (processed directly by PayPal/our payment processor — we do not store your full card number)

**When you browse our site:**
- Standard analytics data (pages visited, device type, approximate location) via Shopify's built-in analytics and, if enabled, Google Analytics
- Cookies for cart functionality and, if you consent, advertising tracking (Google Ads, Meta/Facebook pixel)

## How we use it

- To process and ship your order
- To email you order updates and tracking information
- To respond to support questions
- To improve the site based on aggregate browsing patterns
- If you've opted in: occasional marketing emails (you can unsubscribe anytime)

## Who we share it with

- **Our fulfillment partner** (CJdropshipping) receives your shipping address and order details to ship your product directly to you
- **Our payment processor** (PayPal) receives payment information to process your transaction
- **Shopify** hosts our store and processes data as our platform provider
- We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties

## Cookies and tracking

We use cookies for:
- Keeping items in your cart
- Basic site analytics
- Advertising conversion tracking (Google Ads, Meta), if you've consented to marketing cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect cart functionality.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
- Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these, email us at **convoysupplyco@gmail.com**.

## Data retention

We keep order records as long as needed for tax, legal, and customer service purposes, generally consistent with standard e-commerce retention practices.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
