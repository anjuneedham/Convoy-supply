## The honest version

We don't hold inventory in a warehouse — each order is sent directly from the manufacturer to you. That means shipping takes longer than a company holding stock, but it also means we can offer these prices with no minimum order. Here's what to actually expect.

## Processing time

Orders are processed within **1–3 business days** before shipping.

## Transit time

Once shipped, delivery typically takes **5–11 business days** within the continental United States.

**Total time from order to delivery: usually 6–14 business days.** We know that's not "free 2-day shipping," and we're not going to pretend it is. If you need something faster than that, this may not be the right store for this order.

## Shipping cost

- **Free shipping on orders over $75**
- Orders under $75: flat rate shown at checkout before you pay

## Multiple items in one order

If you order more than one product (including a bundle like the Cab Reset Kit or Full Rig Kit), items ship **separately, from separate shipments** and may arrive on different days. This is normal — you're not missing part of your order if one piece arrives before another.

## Tracking

You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships. If you don't see it within 3 business days of ordering, check your spam folder, then email us.

## Where we ship

Currently: **United States only.**

## You're not home — we know

Most of our customers are on the road when their package arrives. At checkout you can ship to your home address for whenever you're back, or to a location that works for your route. If you're not sure what will work, email us before you order and we'll help you sort it out.

## Delays

Occasionally a shipment runs past the estimated window — weather, customs, or a carrier delay. If your order is significantly late, email us and we'll look into it. We won't promise a delivery date we can't control, but we will tell you what we know.
